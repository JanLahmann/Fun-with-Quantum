# Visibility concept — Fun with Quantum family

Drafted 2026-10-01 with Jan. Status: concept, nothing executed yet unless marked.

## Where we stand (Umami, Sep 2026)

- **Portal** fun-with-quantum.org: ~52 visitors / 30 days — a small hub, few arrive there first.
- **Member sites** get their traffic from search (Google *and* Bing — Bing sends a surprising share).
- **doQumentation** is by far the largest audience (~2,500 visitors Mar–Sep 2026, 18 locales; top
  locales after English: ja, es, uk, de, it, fr).
- Cross-traffic via the family footer is small but real.

So the levers are: search engines, doQumentation as a feeder, and the places where each project's
audience already is.

## Family-wide

1. **doQumentation as the feeder.** A small "Play it: Quantum Coin Game" box on matching
   tutorials (Hello World, superposition, CHSH, …). Cheapest traffic we have.
2. **SEO basics.** The portal has no `sitemap.xml` / `robots.txt` yet — add on all sites, register
   all domains in **Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster Tools**, submit sitemaps. Check
   titles, descriptions, OG images (the family audit covers OG/icons already).
3. **Qiskit ecosystem listings** for the members not listed yet: CertiQ, Quantego, Qutie,
   Qoffee-Maker, the portal. (Listed: doQumentation, Entangible, QuBins, RasQberry, QAMPoser. The
   "Qoffee" entry e-8f32b728 is someone else's project.) Each listing links back and gives a badge.
   Submission via the Qiskit/ecosystem issue form from Jan's account; Claude drafts the entries.
4. **LinkedIn umbrella page** (kit still open) — every post with UTM
   (`utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<slug>`, read on the "Family: campaigns" board).
5. **Launch moment for the browser Coin Game** (currently unlisted at /preview/coin-game/): Show HN,
   r/QuantumComputing, Qiskit Slack, LinkedIn — same day, UTM-tagged.

listings. Then LinkedIn and the Coin Game launch (Jan posts; Claude prepares drafts).
