Build a circuit by hand and see what it does: QAMPoser is a web composer where the
probabilities, the Q-sphere and the histogram follow every gate you place. It is also a
**library**: drop the composer into your own app and use the circuit as the controller for a
game or the centerpiece of a tutorial — exactly how Quantum Runner and Entangible are built.
Created with the QAMP-62 team.
