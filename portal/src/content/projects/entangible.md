Lay gate tiles on a board and watch the circuit come alive: a camera recognizes the layout,
the big screen shows the circuit and its measurement results as you build. Made for booths,
classrooms and kitchen tables — print the kit, or use the on-screen boards. Its **Quantina**
mode lets the circuit's probabilities pick a drink, the grown-up successor of the Qoffee-Maker.
